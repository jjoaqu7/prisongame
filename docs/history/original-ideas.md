# Original ideas

History. The current direction lives in the [design notes](../README.md). This file preserves the earlier concept, including labor quotas and the unit-based possibility. First-person play is now selected; how the earlier quota, multiplier, and global-upgrade ideas fit that direction is still open.

## What I am trying to do

A prison game where you do labor, earn money from your captors, and buy upgrades and multipliers to increase what you earn. You need to make a certain amount to progress to the next level or escape. As you progress, you collect pieces that combine into different escape routes. Between shifts, you can talk to inmates, gain reputation/aura, trade higher-value items, and build control of a gang, eventually extending that control to the entire prison and earning income from it.

CloverPit is a reference for the prison/escape-room idea. Schedule I is a reference for the minimalist, simple cartoon style. A unit-based game is a possibility being considered. Development would involve me, Codex, and MCP.

This overview summarizes my ideas from the [first conversation](first-conversation.md). The passages below preserve my original wording, including spelling and punctuation. Added notes identify questions still open; the assistant's suggestions in that conversation are not settled design decisions.

## Decisions still needed

- Whether gameplay is unit based, and what the player directly controls.

- How pieces are obtained and combined, and how an escape is completed.
